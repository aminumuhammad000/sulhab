Learning & Development

Overview & Purpose

Learning & Development focuses on creating accessible, structured and practical learning opportunities that help individuals develop relevant skills, build confidence and reach their potential.

At Sulhab Creative, we believe education transforms lives when it pairs real-world relevance with structured, human-centred guidance. Rather than passive instruction, our focus is grounded in practical application, supportive mentorship, and clear milestones.

Sulhab Creative Academy

Sulhab Creative Academy is the primary learning initiative operating under Learning & Development. It provides structured learning cohorts, hands-on assignments, direct mentorship, constructive feedback, and guided personal and technical development.

  • Structured Curriculum: Practical modules designed to build competencies required for modern employment and enterprise.
  • Hands-on Assignments & Mentorship: Real project work paired with individual feedback from experienced practitioners.
  • Dedicated Academy Platform: The actual course catalogue, detailed course specifications, and student portals live on the dedicated Academy platform.

Application Pathways

1. Regular Enrolment

Learners apply directly and pay the applicable course fee for open cohorts when scheduled.

2. Scholarship Application

Learners may apply when a scholarship opportunity is announced. Scholarships may be fully or partially sponsored depending on the terms of specific cohorts.

3. Sponsor a Learner

Individuals and partner organisations can support high-potential learners facing financial barriers through structured sponsorship pathways.
