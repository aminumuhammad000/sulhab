Strategic Area 03

Women's Enterprise &
Economic Empowerment

Equipping women with practical knowledge, skills, resources and support to strengthen their businesses, improve their economic opportunities and build sustainable livelihoods.

Purpose & Philosophy

Women's Enterprise & Economic Empowerment focuses on equipping women with practical knowledge, skills, resources and support to strengthen their businesses, improve their economic opportunities and build sustainable livelihoods.

Rather than generic enterprise training, this strategic area directly addresses the operational, marketing, and commercial realities that female entrepreneurs encounter daily. The objective is stability, resilience, and sustainable economic independence.

Target Audience

The primary audience consists of women who already own or operate businesses, including:

  • Small Business Owners & Traders: Female founders running active, existing micro- and small-scale operations seeking stability and efficiency.
  • Informal & Registered Enterprises: Women looking to transition from informal trade toward formalised records, improved margins, and structured operations.
  • Note on Focus: This strategic area is specifically tailored to strengthening active enterprises rather than functioning as an unguided start-a-business incubator.

Practical Development Areas

Pricing, Costing & Financial Education

Practical numeracy, record-keeping, separating business from household expenses, and establishing healthy, sustainable profit margins.

Branding & Product Presentation

Elevating customer perception, thoughtful packaging, consistent presentation, and communicating value effectively.

Digital Marketing & Customer Reach

Leveraging accessible digital platforms and messaging apps to connect with wider audiences and enhance repeat sales.

Peer Support & Mentorship

Fostering supportive peer circles and experienced mentorship networks where women exchange practical solutions and encouragement.
