Who We Are

A Learning, Development & Impact Organisation

Sulhab Creative is a learning, development, innovation and impact organisation. We exist to help people turn their potential into real skills, meaningful opportunities and positive change.

By offering accessible, structured frameworks across four unified strategic areas, we support learners, job seekers, entrepreneurs, and community leaders in creating lasting, sustainable transformation.

Our Mission

To bridge gaps in learning, skills and opportunities by providing guided education, mentorship, practical development and support for innovation, so people can grow, build livelihoods and contribute to their communities.

Our Vision

A world where people and communities have the knowledge, skills, support and opportunities they need to reach their potential, improve their lives and help build a better future.

Purpose & Core Values

To help people turn their potential into real skills, meaningful opportunities and positive change.

  • Practical and grounded learning
  • Guided mentorship & personal support
  • Inclusivity & sustainable livelihoods
  • Ethical collaboration & measurable impact
